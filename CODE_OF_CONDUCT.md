# Code of Conduct

MetricSpec is a technical project for people testing analytical meaning. Keep
discussion respectful, specific, and focused on the work.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct without being hostile.
- Assume contributors are acting in good faith.
- Critique code, docs, and designs rather than people.
- Keep public issues free of credentials, production data, secrets, and private
  customer context.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions after maintainers ask to move on.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may restrict participation
for behavior that harms the project. Report conduct concerns privately to the
maintainers once the repository is public.
